## 🚫 100% PURE STORY REEL RULES (STRICT & ABSOLUTE)

1. **NO Scanlation Watermarks**: Any `ASURASCANS.COM`, scanlation logo diamonds, or credit text MUST be cropped or painted out with matching background tones.
2. **NO Platform Branding**: Zero YouTube logos, zero Instagram stamps, zero TikTok watermarks inside the video.
3. **NO Call-To-Action Cards**: Never end the video with "Read on AsuraScans", "Chapter X Available Now", or Discord invitation banners.
4. **Pure Story Climax**: Every video MUST conclude with a **pure story cliffhanger** (a character vow, an unanswered threat, or a high-stakes dramatic revelation).
