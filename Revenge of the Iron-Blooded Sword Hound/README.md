# Revenge of the Iron-Blooded Sword Hound (철혈검가 사냥개의 회귀)
## Automated Viral Short / Reel Production Standard

---

### 📖 1. Series Lore & Narrative DNA
* **Protagonist:** Vikir Baskerville (비키르 바스커빌)
* **Archetype:** Ruthless Regressor / Cold Tactical Hunting Dog / Clan Destroyer
* **Core Conflict:** Vikir dedicated his entire life doing dirty, bloody wetwork as the loyal "hunting dog" of the Iron-Blooded Baskerville clan. When his usefulness ended, his own ruthless patriarch, Hugo Baskerville, framed him for treason and sent him to the guillotine. 
* **The Regression:** As the guillotine blade severed his neck, Vikir regressed 40 years back to the moment of his birth as a Baskerville infant. Retaining every sword technique, mana circulation method, poison resistance, and encyclopedic knowledge of clan secrets, Vikir resolves to become a hound that no longer hunts for others—but one that bites the throat of Baskerville and tears it down from the foundation.

```

[16.0s - 24.5s] Phase 6: Climax / Ruthless Dominance (Passing the sword cradle trial / terrifying patriarch Hugo)
[24.5s - 27.5s] Ending: PURE STORY CLIFFHANGER (Vikir's inner vow to destroy Baskerville)
```

* **Glass Container:** Rounded dark rectangle (`rgba(10, 10, 20, 0.85)`), radius `24px`, outline with 2px crimson/gold accent.
* **Beat Drop FX:** 
  * Blinding white flash (`alpha 0.95 -> 0.0` over 6 frames).
  * Sinusoidal screen shake (`45px` amplitude decaying over 20 frames).
